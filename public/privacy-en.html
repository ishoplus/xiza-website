Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the Xitto iOS mobile application and the Xiza AI Agent Services website (collectively, "the Service"), operated by Yu Hsiang Wang.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the following personal information:

  • Email address — used for account verification and login.
  • Username — used to identify your account.

All other data generated within the Xitto app — including conversation history, AI memory, habit logs, and dietary records — is stored locally on your device only and is never uploaded to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your account.
  • Enabling cross-device account access.
  • Processing subscriptions via Apple In-App Purchase.
  • Responding to technical support requests.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.

Limited exceptions include:

  • RevenueCat — used to process in-app purchases and validate subscriptions. Only the minimum necessary transaction data is shared.
  • Apple — payments made through the Apple App Store are processed directly by Apple and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law or a legitimate government request.

4. AI Features and Data Processing

Xitto supports multiple AI backends:

  • On-device models — all conversations are processed entirely on your device and never sent to any server.
  • Cloud AI models — if you choose to use a third-party cloud AI (e.g., OpenAI, MiniMax), your conversation content will be processed in accordance with that provider's privacy policy.
  • Pro plan — AI requests are relayed through our proxy server. We do not store conversation content.

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained until you choose to delete your account. You can delete your account at any time from the app's Settings. Upon deletion, all server-side data associated with your account is permanently removed immediately.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ishoplus@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: ishoplus@gmail.com
